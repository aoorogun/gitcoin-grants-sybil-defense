# Interview guide

“This is a 2022-context Gitcoin project based on the public GR14 governance brief. I preserved its aggregate statistics but generated privacy-safe contribution graphs rather than claiming access to internal wallet labels. Donor features capture bursts, concentration, wallet maturity, trust and shared fingerprints. A supervised model and Isolation Forest prioritise review; humans and appeals remain central. The allocation engine recomputes quadratic funding under baseline and proposed risk weights, reconciles the matching pool exactly, and shows which grants move. This separates model quality from the actual mechanism impact.”

Discuss why random splits leak coordinated campaigns; why false positives harm pluralism; how survey labels and heuristics can create feedback loops; why quadratic matching amplifies identity attacks; and why settlement must be deterministic, reproducible and governed.
